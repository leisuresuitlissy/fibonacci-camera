Drag inside the guide to move it, a corner to size it, the top handle to rotate it. Double-tap steps through the eight orientations. The lock hides the handles and holds everything in place. In the viewfinder a pinch zooms the lens. On a photo there is no lens, so two fingers belong to the guide instead: pinch to resize it, twist to rotate it.

Proportion changes how fast the spiral opens out, which is what makes it tall or wide. At φ it is the golden spiral. Anywhere else it is still a true spiral, just a different one — nothing is ever stretched, so the curve stays a curve. Fit the frame finds the proportion whose natural shape matches this photo.
